# Glossary: T

## t

**Definition**: butch lesbian

**Notes**: from "tomboy"

## taimei (太妹)

**Definition**: female delinquent, rebellious girl, bad girl

## tangjie, cousin (堂姐)

**Definition**: Ju Jingyi

**Notes**: 堂姐 (tángjiě) = cousin. from 明星殿堂一姐 (míngxīng diàntáng yī jiě) = Star Palace first sister

## taoba (桃叭), tb

**Definition**: Taoba (桃叭), a Chinese fundraising platform

**Notes**: 桃叭 (táo bā) = Taoba

## taopu (桃浦), tao (桃), set (套)

**Definition**: having high popularity or status, being at the top of something

**Notes**: from "top"

## taoying (陶莺), fruit manager (水果总)

**Definition**: Tao Ying (陶莺), the CEO and co-founder of Siba Media

## tcl

**Definition**: too sad, too miserable

**Notes**: 太惨了 (tài cǎn le) = too miserable

## team (队, 队伍)

**Definition**: an organized grouping of 48 Group members that perform and conduct activities together

**Notes**: 队伍 (duìwǔ) = team

## team gii, gii, gⅡ, team g2 (g2 队), g2, jitu (极兔)

**Definition**: CGT48 Team GII

**Notes**: 极兔 (jí tù) sounds like "G two"

## team hii, hii, team h2, h2, hⅡ, team h (h 队), h, aichitu (爱吃兔), love rabbit (爱吃兔)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team HII

**Notes**: 爱吃兔 (ài chī tù) sounds like "H two"

## team nii, nii, nⅡ, team n2, n2, team n (n 队), n, en (恩), entu (恩兔), en rabbit (恩兔)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team NII

**Notes**: 恩兔 (ēn tù) sounds like "N two"

## team niii, niii, nⅢ, team n3 (n3 队), n3, nnn, ensui (恩穗)

**Definition**: GNZ48 Team NIII

**Notes**: 恩穗 (ēn suì) sounds like "N three"

## team push (队推), team oshi, team fan

**Definition**: supporting or being a fan of a specific team in an idol group

**Notes**: 队推 (duì tuī) = team push

## team sii, sii, sⅡ, team s2, s2, team s (s 队), s, aisitu (艾斯兔), ace rabbit (艾斯兔)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team SII

**Notes**: 艾斯兔 (àisī tù) sounds like "S two"

## team x (x 队), x, fork (叉), fork team (叉队)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team X

**Notes**: 叉队 (chā duì) = X team

## team xii, xii, xⅡ, team x2, x2, fork rabbit (叉兔)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team XII

**Notes**: 叉兔 (chā tù) sounds like “X two”

## tearing (撕, 撕逼)

**Definition**: fight, catfight

**Notes**: from 撕屄 (sī bī) = cunt fight

## tencent (腾讯), tx, goose (鹅), 🐧

**Definition**: Tencent (腾讯)

**Notes**: 腾讯 (téng xùn) = Tencent. Tencent's logo is a penguin, and the Chinese word for penguin (企鹅) contains the character for goose (鹅)

## tengshao (滕少), rocky

**Definition**: Teng Shao (滕少) a.k.a. ROCKY, SNH48's music director and producer

## theater party (剧场党), offline party (线下党)

**Definition**: fans who attend theater shows and events in person

**Notes**: 剧场党 (jùchǎng dǎng) = theater party, theater community member

## thigh hugging (抱大腿)

**Definition**: to suck up to someone, to try to curry favor with someone

**Notes**: 抱大腿 (bào dàtuǐ) = hug thighs. from people hugging the thighs of a Buddha statue while praying for help

## tiancai, day dish (天菜)

**Definition**: one's ideal type in terms of appearance or personality

## tianmei (甜美, 甜妹), sweet girl (甜妹)

**Definition**: a girl whose appearance and personality is cute, feminine, and unaggressive

**Notes**: 甜妹 (tián mèi) = sweet girl, sweet sister

## tiantian xiangshang, day day up (天天向上)

**Definition**: improve day by day

**Notes**: from the phrase 好好學習，天天向上 (hǎohǎo xuéxí, tiāntiān xiàngshàng) = study hard and improve day by day. literally translated as "good good study, day day up"

## tieba, post bar (贴吧), bar (吧)

**Definition**: Baidu Tieba (百度贴吧), a Chinese forum platform

**Notes**: 贴吧 (tiēba) = post bar

## tla, talk (谈)

**Definition**: dating, romance, falling in love

**Notes**: 谈恋爱 (tán liàn ài) = fall in love

## tmd

**Definition**: fuck, damn it

**Notes**: 他妈的 (tā mā de) = damn it

## tmi

**Definition**: "too much information" (often used to talk about one's daily life or otherwise useless trivia)

**Notes**: from "too much information"

## tony, tony laoshi (tony 老师)

**Definition**: hairstylist, barber

**Notes**: from hairdressing chain TONI&GUY

## tool (工具), tool man (工具人)

**Definition**: someone used by another person as a tool or as a means to an end

**Notes**: 工具人 (gōngjù rén) = tool man, tool person

## top, tops

**Definition**: the top 48 Group members in terms of rank or popularity

**Notes**: from "top members"

## tpl, tp, ptl

**Definition**: a romantic relationship involving a butch lesbian and a femme lesbian

**Notes**: from "T and P love" or "P and T love"

## tql

**Definition**: impressive, awesome

**Notes**: 太强了 (tài qiáng le) = too strong

## transitional performance, transition gongyan (过渡公演), waiting stage

**Definition**: a theater performance used while "waiting" for new original stages

**Notes**: 过渡公演 (guòdù gōngyǎn) = transition performance

## trend, general trend (大势)

**Definition**: current situation, current state of affairs

**Notes**: 大势 = general trend

## ttl

**Definition**: a romantic relationship involving two butch lesbians

**Notes**: from "T and T love"

## ttl

**Definition**: too sweet, so sweet

**Notes**: 太甜了 (tài tián le) = too sweet

## tu bang shen qi, god seven slaughter list (屠榜神七)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team NII members occupying every rank from #1 to #7 at the 4th SNH48 Group general election in 2017

## tubang, slaughter list (屠榜)

**Definition**: something surpassing or replacing something else on a leaderboard, chart, or ranking list

**Notes**: 屠榜 (túbǎng) = slaughtering the charts

## tucao (吐槽)

**Definition**: complaining about or being dissatisfied with something

## tui, push (推), oshi, oshimen

**Definition**: a fan's favorite idol, the idol they "push" (support)

**Notes**: 推 (tuī) = push. from the Japanese 推し (oshi), 推しメン (oshimen) = member you support

## twice-cooked pork (回锅肉)

**Definition**: an idol who has already debuted or has already been on a survival show

**Notes**: 回锅肉 (huíguōròu) = twice-cooked pork

## txl, tx

**Definition**: gay, gay person, homosexual

**Notes**: 同性恋 (tóng xìng liàn) = homosexuality
