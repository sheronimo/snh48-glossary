# Glossary: D

## d7, d7b, d7boys, dsb, d7 youth league (D7 少年团)

**Definition**: D7BOYS, a boy group under Star48

**Notes**: also known as Double Seven Boys, D 七少年团

## dahao, tuba (大号)

**Definition**: main account, primary social media account

**Notes**: 大号 (dà hào) = tuba

## daiwang, bag king, pocket king (袋王)

**Definition**: Pocket48 admin team

**Notes**: 袋王 (dài wáng) = pocket king

## dalao, dl, boss, big guy (大佬), boss fan, big fan (大佬粉)

**Definition**: wealthy fans who spend large amounts of money on votes and gifts for idols

**Notes**: 大佬 (dàlǎo) = big guy, big boss, gangster

## dan tui wang, single push king, dtw (单推王)

**Definition**: the highest-spending fan of an idol. an idol can only have one DTW at a time

**Notes**: 单推王 (dān tuī wáng) = single push king

## danfan (但凡)

**Definition**: SNH48 friend group consisting of Liu Lifei, Shen Mengyao, Zhang Xin, and Zheng Danni

## danmei (耽美), yaoi, boys love, bl

**Definition**: media focused on male homosexuality or intimate relationships between male characters

**Notes**: 耽美 (dānměi) = indulge in beauty. from the Japanese 耽美 (tanbi) = aesthetics, pursuit of beauty

## danmu, barrage (彈幕)

**Definition**: a barrage of comments superimposed over a video (often seen on Bilibili)

**Notes**: from the Japanese 弾幕 (danmaku) = bullet curtain

## danso, menswear (男装)

**Definition**: a woman cross-dressing as a man

**Notes**: from the Japanese 男装 (dansou) = woman disguised as a man

## dantui, dt, single push, only push (单推), tan oshi

**Definition**: a fan who only supports one idol

**Notes**: 单推 (dān tuī) = single push

## dark funds, dark accounts (暗账)

**Definition**: fundraising that is conduced privately and cannot be publicly tracked

**Notes**: 暗账 (àn zhàng) = dark accounts

## dark horse

**Definition**: unpopular trainee who gains popularity towards the end

**Notes**: survival show

## dark votes, secret votes (暗票)

**Definition**: votes that are casted privately and cannot be publicly tracked

**Notes**: 暗票 (àn piào) = dark votes

## data (数据), doing data (做数据)

**Definition**: increasing an artist's digital presence via statistics such as view counts, rankings, likes, and comments

**Notes**: 做数据 (zuò shùjù) = do data

## dbl

**Definition**: go bankrupt, close down

**Notes**: 倒闭了 (dǎo bì le) = shut down

## dbq

**Definition**: sorry

**Notes**: 对不起 (duì bù qǐ) = I am sorry

## dd, up, top (顶), ding ding (顶顶), go up (上去)

**Definition**: bump (increasing another post's online visibility by moving it to the top of a discussion feed)

**Notes**: 顶顶 (dǐng dǐng) = up up

## dfxr

**Definition**: slut-shaming

**Notes**: 荡妇羞辱 (dàng fù xiū rù) = slut-shaming

## diantai, radio stream, radio live (电台)

**Definition**: audio-only livestream

**Notes**: 电台 (diàntái) = radio station

## dishao, low fever (低烧)

**Definition**: 低烧 ("Low Fever"), a GNZ48 song

**Notes**: 低烧 (dīshāo) = low fever

## diss

**Definition**: insulting or speaking disrepectfully to someone

**Notes**: from "diss"

## dk uniform (dk 制服), dk

**Definition**: Japanese high school boys’ school uniforms

## doi

**Definition**: having sex

**Notes**: from "do" + 爱 (ài) = love. 爱 sounds like "i" in English, so do 爱 = "doing it" or "making love"

## douban (豆瓣), db, green software

**Definition**: Douban (豆瓣)

**Notes**: 豆瓣 (dòu bàn) = Douban

## double, pair (双)

**Definition**: bisexual

**Notes**: 双 (shuāng) = double

## double arrow (双箭头)

**Definition**: two people having mutual crushes on or unrequited love for each other

**Notes**: 双箭头 (shuāng jiàntóu) = double-headed arrow

## douyin (抖音, 抖 🎵), vibrato (抖音) dy

**Definition**: Douyin (抖音), the Chinese version of TikTok

**Notes**: 抖音 (dǒuyīn) = Douyin

## draft (选秀), xianxiu (选秀)

**Definition**: survival show, talent show

**Notes**: 选秀 (xuǎnxiù) = draft

## drainage (引流, 排水)

**Definition**: transfer point for money, traffic, or commerce

**Notes**: 引流 (yǐnliú), 排水 (páishuǐ) = drainage

## dream girl (梦女), yumejoshi, yumejo

**Definition**: female fans who fantasize about being in a relationship with (usually male) characters or idols

**Notes**: from the Japanese 夢女子 (yume joshi) = dreaming girl

## dsn

**Definition**: Disney, Disneyland

**Notes**: from 迪士尼 (díshìní) = Disney
