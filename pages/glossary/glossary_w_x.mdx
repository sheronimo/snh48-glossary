## w, -w

**Definition**: 10,000, multiples of 10,000 (example: 5w = 50,000)

**Notes**: from 万 (wàn) = ten thousand

## w, w-, double (双)

**Definition**: two people with a similar attribute paired together. (example: Liu Lifei and Liu Qianqian together are known as "wLiu")

**Notes**: 双 (shuāng) = double, pair, two of. from "w" as in "double u".

## waiber waiber (歪 ber 歪 ber), wiper wiper

**Definition**: misspelled "viper viper" lyrics from In the Dark, a SNH48 song

**Notes**: 歪 ber (wāi ber) and "wiper" sound like "viper"

## waiwu, foreign affairs (外务)

**Definition**: external schedule (non-SNH48 activities, such as filming and interviews)

**Notes**: 外务 (wàiwù) = foreign affairs

## wajijiwa (哇唧唧哇), wjjw, 🐸🐔🐔🐸

**Definition**: WAJIJIWA Entertainment (哇唧唧哇), a Chinese entertainment company

**Notes**: 哇唧唧哇 (wa jī jī wa) sounds like "蛙鸡鸡蛙" (wā jī jī wā) = "frog chicken chicken frog"

## wanghong (网红)

**Definition**: Internet celebrity, influencer, someone who became famous via the Internet

**Notes**: from 网络红人 (wǎngluò hóngrén) = Internet celebrity

## wangjing (王靖), wj

**Definition**: Wang Jing (王靖), the chairman of Siba Media and Wang Zijie's sister

## wangzhe rongyao, king of glory, wzry (王者荣耀)

**Definition**: Honor of Kings (王者荣耀), a popular Chinese mobile game

**Notes**: 王者荣耀 (wáng zhě róng yào)= Honor of Kings

## washing feet (洗脚), foot-washing maid (洗脚婢)

**Definition**: idols with low popularity relative to the other idols in their group or team

**Notes**: 洗脚婢 (xǐ jiǎo bì) = foot-washing maid

## water (水着), mizugi

**Definition**: swimsuit, bikini

**Notes**: from the Japanese 水着 (mizugi) = swimsuit, swimwear

## water single, water order (水着单), summer single, swimsuit single, mizugi single

**Definition**: SNH48's yearly single where all members selected for the title track wear swimsuits

**Notes**: from the Japanese 水着 (mizugi) = swimsuit, swimwear

## wdcc

**Definition**: a new idol quickly becoming popular and overtaking their seniors

**Notes**: 弯道超车 (wān dào chāo chē) = overtaking on a bend

## wechat, weixin (微信), wx, vx, 🛰️

**Definition**: WeChat (微信), a Chinese social messaging platform

**Notes**: WeChat is called Weixin (微信) in Chinese, but the V and W sounds are homophonic in some dialects

## weibo, microblog (微博) wb, vb, 🧣

**Definition**: Weibo (微博), a Chinese social media platform

**Notes**: 微博 (wēi bó) = Weibo

## weidian (微店), weidian (wei 店), wd, vd

**Definition**: Weidian (微店), a Chinese shopping and e-commerce platform

**Notes**: 微店 (wēi diàn) = Weidian

## weifen, wf, solo fan, only powder (唯粉), only rice (唯饭)

**Definition**: a fan who only likes or supports one member of an idol group

**Notes**: 唯粉 (wéi fěn) = only fan

## weiquan, wq, rights protection (维权)

**Definition**: fans formally protesting an idol's mistreatment by their company

**Notes**: 维权 (wéiquán) = defend legal rights

## welfare (福利)

**Definition**: sexually explicit material

**Notes**: 福利 (fúlì) = welfare, benefits

## welfare princess, fuli ji, flj (福利姬)

**Definition**: women that create sexual content while wearing cosplay

**Notes**: from the Japanese 姬 (yume) = princess

## white lotus (白莲花), blh

**Definition**: someone who has an innocent appearance but is secretly evil

**Notes**: 白莲花 (bái lián huā) = white lotus

## white-eyed wolf (白眼狼)

**Definition**: ingrate, ungrateful person

**Notes**: 白眼狼 (báiyǎn láng) = white-eyed wolf

## wind review (风评)

**Definition**: rumor, gossip (usually about someone's reputation)

**Notes**: 风评 (fēng píng) = wind review

## wind review victimization (风评被害)

**Definition**: to suffer reputational damage as a result of rumors

**Notes**: from the Japanese 風評被害 (fuuhyou higai) = reputation damage caused by rumors

## winter (冬天)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group's Best 50 concert, the Best 50 voting period

**Notes**: 冬天 (dōngtiān) = winter

## winter is coming (冬天快到了)

**Definition**: "Best 50 season is soon approaching"

**Notes**: 冬天快到了 (dōngtiān kuài dàole) = winter is coming

## wma, wmom (w 妈)

**Definition**: two idols' respective sets of solo fans, usually due to a fight between them. often specifically refers to Shen Mengyao fans and Yuan Yiqi fans

**Notes**: w 妈 (w mā) = double mom

## woc

**Definition**: WTF, what the fuck

**Notes**: 臥槽 (wò cáo) = "what the fuck". also 卧槽

## wood (木头), wooden man (木头人)

**Definition**: blockhead, someone who acts dull or dense

**Notes**: 木头人 (mùtou rén) = wooden man

## wota art (wota 艺), otagei (ota 艺, 御宅艺), wotagei

**Definition**: synchronized dancing and cheering performed by otaku (usually with light sticks)

**Notes**: from the Japanese ヲタ芸 (wotagei) = wota art

## wpg

**Definition**: CP fan that is biased towards or prefers one person in a CP, general bias towards someone

**Notes**: 歪屁股 (wāi pìgu) = crooked butt (a CP fan whose center of gravity is off)

## wsn, ws

**Definition**: man who has disgusting attitidues towards women

**Notes**: 猥琐男 (wěi suǒ nán) = wretched man

## x man, x eel (x 鳗), x-man, fork eel (叉鳗)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team X fans

**Notes**: x 鳗 (x mán) = X eel, which sounds like "X-Men". 叉 (chā) = fork, cross, X

## x2 turtle (x2 龟), little green turtle (小绿龟)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team XII fans

**Notes**: x2 龟 (x2 guī) = XII turtle. 小绿龟 (xiǎo lǜ guī) = little green turtle

## xdl

**Definition**: I learned something

**Notes**: 学到了 (xué dào le) = learned

## xiangsheng, cross-talk, comic dialogue (相声)

**Definition**: traditional Chinese comedy style involving quick banter on stage

**Notes**: 相声 (xiàngsheng) = cross-talk

## xiaohao, side account, small account, trumpet (小号), 🎺

**Definition**: side account, secondary or personal social media account

**Notes**: 小号 (xiǎo hào) = small account

## xiaohongshu (小红书, 小 🔴 书), xhs, little red book (小红书), red, little 🍠 (小 🍠), 🍠

**Definition**: Xiaohongshu (小红书), a Chinese social media and e-commerce platform

**Notes**: 小红书 (xiǎo hóng shū) = Xiaohongshu

## xin en (新恩), new n

**Definition**: the iteration of SNH48 Team NII from around 2018 to the present day

**Notes**: 新恩 (xīn ēn) = new N

## xiong, 🐻

**Definition**: breasts

**Notes**: 胸 (xiōng) = chest, breasts. 🐻 is from 熊 (xióng) = bear, which sounds similar to 胸

## xiongtao (熊韬), xiongzong, mr. xiong (熊总)

**Definition**: Xiong Tao (熊韬), the general manager of CGT48 and vice president of Meta48

**Notes**: 熊总 (xióng zǒng) = Mr. Xiong, Boss Xiong

## xiufen, show fan (秀粉)

**Definition**: survival show fan

**Notes**: 秀粉 (xiù fěn) = show fan

## xjj

**Definition**: young lady, pretty girl

**Notes**: 小姐姐 (xiǎo jiě jiě) = little sister

## xls, trainee (训练生)

**Definition**: trainee, an idol that has not yet debuted

**Notes**: 训练生 (xùnliàn shēng) = trainee

## xql

**Definition**: cute couple, pair of sweethearts (often used with CPs)

**Notes**: 小情侣 (xiǎo qínglǚ) = little couple

## xsr

**Definition**: sexual harassment

**Notes**: 性骚扰

## xswl

**Definition**: LMAO

**Notes**: 笑死我了 (xiào sǐ wǒ le) = I laughed to death

## xxj, primary school chicken (小学鸡, 小学 🐔)

**Definition**: immature/childish person

**Notes**: 小学鸡 (xiǎo xué jī) = elementary school chicken. derogatory term

## xxs

**Definition**: behaving in a very childish, naive, or immature way

**Notes**: 小学生 (xiǎo xuéshēng) = primary school student
