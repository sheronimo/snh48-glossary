# Glossary: G

## gachi 恋 (gachi love), gachi 粉 (gachi fan), gachikoi, gachi

**Definition**: fans who are romantically in love with their idol

**Notes**: from the Japanese ガチ恋 (gachikoi) = seriously in love

## galaxy battleship (银河战舰)

**Definition**: a top-class team made of a lineup of superstars, SNH48 Team HII

**Notes**: from "Galácticos"

## gao chao (高朝), gc

**Definition**: orgasm

**Notes**: 高潮 (gāocháo) = high tide, climax

## gay honey (gay 蜜)

**Definition**: gay male friend of a woman

**Notes**: from "gay" and 闺蜜 (guīmì) = close female friend

## gay3, gay4, gay5, gay6, gay7

**Definition**: SNH48 Group live performance control operators (responsible for lighting, music, timing, etc.)

**Notes**: gay3 refers to SNH48's stage operators. gay4 refers to GNZ48's, gay5 refers to BEJ48's, gay6 refers to SHY48's, gay7 refers to CKG48's

## geida (给达), gaydar(给达, gay 达)

**Definition**: gaydar, the ability to perceive whether someone is gay

**Notes**: from "gay" and 雷达 (léidá) = radar

## get

**Definition**: understand

**Notes**: from "get it"

## giao

**Definition**: Internet meme catchphrase (no actual meaning)

**Notes**: from Internet celebrity Giao 哥 and his catchphrase "一 gay 我里 giaogiao"

## giegie, geigei

**Definition**: humorous misspelling of "gege" (older brother)

**Notes**: Internet meme

## ginseng, ancient ginseng (古参), kosan

**Definition**: veteran fan, someone who has followed something for a long time

**Notes**: from the Japanese 古参 (kosan) = seniority, old-timer

## girlfriend fan, girlfriend powder (女友粉)

**Definition**: female fans who regard themselves as their idol's girlfriend and treat their idol as such

**Notes**: 女友粉 (nǚyǒu fěn) = girlfriend fan

## gkd

**Definition**: hurry up and get something done

**Notes**: 搞快点 (gǎo kuài diǎn) = hurry up

## gnz48, gnz, jianazhen, cannes town (戛纳镇), juenzei (居恩贼)

**Definition**: GNZ48, a sister group of SNH48 based in Guangzhou

**Notes**: from "Guangzhou 48". 戛纳镇 (jiá nà zhèn) and 居恩贼 (jū ēn zéi) sound like "GNZ"

## gongyan, public performance (公演), gy, stage, theater stage

**Definition**: theater performance

**Notes**: 公演 (gōngyǎn) = performance

## gouba (狗芭, 🐶 芭), shitba

**Definition**: Siba Media (derogatory term)

**Notes**: from 狗 (gǒu) = dog, and 丝芭 (sībā) = Siba

## graduation (毕业), grad, sotsugyou

**Definition**: an idol leaving or retiring from their idol group

**Notes**: from the Japanese 卒業 (sotsugyou) = graduation

## greasy (油腻)

**Definition**: acting sleazy or unpleasant

**Notes**: 油腻 (yóunì) = greasy

## green tea (绿茶), green tea bitch (绿茶婊), tea (茶), 🍵

**Definition**: a woman who appears innocent but is secretly ambitious and manipulative

**Notes**: 绿茶婊 (lǜchá biǎo) = green tea bitch

## group push (团推), box push (箱推), group fan (团饭), hako oshi

**Definition**: a fan that supports or likes all members of an idol group

**Notes**: from the Japanese 箱推し (hako oshi) = group oshi

## gua, melon (瓜), 🍉

**Definition**: male/masculine appearance or role

**Notes**: often used with "1" or when discussing CPs or member personalities

## gua, melon (瓜), 🍉

**Definition**: rumor, gossip

**Notes**: 瓜 (guā) = melon. from 八卦 (bāguà) = gossip

## guangba (广芭), gb, guangzhou branch (广州分团)

**Definition**: GNZ48 management

**Notes**: 广州丝芭文化传媒有限公司 = Guangzhou Siba Culture Media Co., Ltd.

## guazhu, melon master, melon owner (瓜主)

**Definition**: person who distributes rumors

**Notes**: 瓜主 (guā zhǔ) = melon master

## guo jianliang (郭建良), gjl, guozong, mr. guo (郭总), gz

**Definition**: Guo Jianliang (郭建良), the general manager of BEJ48

**Notes**: 郭总 (guō zǒng) = Mr. Guo, Boss Guo

## guofeng, national style, country style (国风)

**Definition**: traditional Chinese cultural style

**Notes**: 国风 (guó fēng) = national style

## guorui (国瑞), gr

**Definition**: CKG48, CKG48's Star Dream Theater

**Notes**: CKG48's theater was located in Chongqing Guorui Center
