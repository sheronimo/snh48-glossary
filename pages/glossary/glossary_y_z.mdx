## yaohuchaohe, shaking pot, shaking pot frying river (摇壶炒河)

**Definition**: China's four major idol groups in 2019 and 2020: UNINE, R1SE, Rocket Girls 101, and SNH48

**Notes**: each character in 摇壶炒河 (yáo hú chǎo hé) references a different idol group: 摇 (UNINE), 壶 (R1SE), 炒 (Rocket Girls 101), 河 (SNH48)

## yedie, night butterfly (夜蝶), oshimeshi

**Definition**: 夜蝶 ("Night Butterfly"), a SNH48 song adapated from the Japanese AKB48 song "Oshibe to Meshibe to Yoru no Chouchou"

**Notes**: 夜蝶 (yè dié) = night butterfly

## yesheng (叶盛), yezong, yz, mr. ye, boss ye, president ye (叶总), laoye (老叶)

**Definition**: Ye Sheng (叶盛), the general manager of SNH48

**Notes**: 叶总 (yè zǒng) = Mr. Ye, Boss Ye, President Ye. 老叶 (lǎo yè) = Old Ye

## yijie, first sister (一姐), second sister (二姐), third sister (三姐), …

**Definition**: SNH48 Group members according to their most recent rank in the general elections (example: the current yijie is Yuan Yiqi)

**Notes**: 一姐 (yī jiě) = first sister, 二姐 (èr jiě) = second sister, 三姐 (sān jiě) = third sister, etc.

## yisi ok (一四 ok)

**Definition**: it's okay, no problem

**Notes**: 一四 ok (yī si OK) sounds like "it's OK"

## yjjc

**Definition**: outstanding skills in a specific area, someone who stands far above their competition

**Notes**: 一骑绝尘 (yī qí jué chén) = to ride so fast that all others are left in the dust

## ylq, entertainment circle (娱乐圈), 🐟circle (🐟 圈)

**Definition**: showbiz, the (Chinese) entertainment industry

**Notes**: 娱乐圈 (yú lè quān) = entertainment industry

## youku (优酷), pants (裤), 👖

**Definition**: Youku (优酷), a Chinese video streaming platform

**Notes**: 裤 (kù) = pants, which sounds like the second character in 优酷 (yōukù)

## youtang (悠唐), ut, castle (城堡), little castle (小城堡)

**Definition**: BEJ48, BEJ48's Star Dream Theater

**Notes**: BEJ48's theater was a castle located in U-Town Mall (悠唐生活广场, Youtang Shenghuo Square)

## yp

**Definition**: booty call, casual sex

**Notes**: 约炮 (yuēpào) = hook-up

## ytsn, hard candy girls (硬糖少女)

**Definition**: Bonbon Girls 303 (硬糖少女 303), a Chinese female idol group

**Notes**: 硬糖少女 303 (yìng táng shàonǚ 303) = Bonbon Girls 303

## yuan en (元恩), ori n, old n

**Definition**: the era of SNH48 Team NII defined by SNH48's 2nd generation members (from 2013 to around 2018)

**Notes**: 元恩 (yuán ēn) = original N

## yuanqi, vitality (元气), yuanqi niang (元气娘)

**Definition**: as lively, cheerful, and beautiful as a stereotypical anime girl

**Notes**: 元气 (yuánqì) = vitality, 元气娘 (yuánqì niáng) = vitality girl. from the Japanese 元気 (genki) = healthy, lively

## yuanqi (园骑), yq, garden rider (园骑)

**Definition**: netizens of Baidu Tieba's 生个女孩吧 (HHY) forum

**Notes**: 园骑 (yuán qí) = garden rider

## yulongcheng (豫珑城), ylc

**Definition**: SHY48, SHY48's Star Dream Theater

**Notes**: SHY48's theater was located in Yulong City, 豫珑城 (yù lóng chéng)

## yusanjia (御三家), ysj, top3

**Definition**: the 3 highest-ranking SNH48 Group members

**Notes**: from the Japanese 御三家 (gosanke) = noble three houses

## yxyy

**Definition**: single-minded, undivided attention

**Notes**: 一心一意 (yī xīn yī yì) = intently

## yyds

**Definition**: forever single

**Notes**: 永远单身 (yǒng yuǎn dān shēn) = forever single

## yyds

**Definition**: GOAT, really amazing

**Notes**: 永远滴神 (yǒng yuǎn dī shén) = eternal god

## yygq, yin and yang, eccentric (阴阳怪气)

**Definition**: being passive-aggressive, vague and sarcastic, or insinuating

**Notes**: 阴阳怪气 (yīn yáng guài qì) = eccentric

## zanxiu, hiatus (暂休)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group members that have effectively graduated from their group and are not currently active as idols, but are still under contract with Siba

**Notes**: 暂休 (zàn xiū) = on hiatus, on break

## zbzy

**Definition**: capitalism

**Notes**: 资本主义 (zīběn zhǔyì) = capitalism

## zc

**Definition**: self-harm

**Notes**: 自残 (zì cán) = self-harm

## zdl

**Definition**: "understood", "I got it"

**Notes**: 知道了 (zhīdàole) = understood

## zhainan, nerd (宅男)

**Definition**: male otaku

**Notes**: 宅男 (zháinán) = male otaku

## zhanju, occupy (占据)

**Definition**: 占据 ("Occupy"), a GNZ48 song

**Notes**: 占据 (zhànjù) = occupy

## zhi, straight (直)

**Definition**: heterosexual

**Notes**: 直 (zhí) = straight

## zhibo (直播), zb

**Definition**: livestream

**Notes**: 直播 (zhíbò) = livestream

## zhifubao, zfb, alipay (支付宝)

**Definition**: Alipay (支付宝), a Chinese payment platform

**Notes**: 支付宝 (zhīfùbǎo) = Alipay

## zhihu, zh, know almost (知乎)

**Definition**: Zhihu (知乎), a Chinese question-and-answer discussion platform

## zhinan, straight man (直男)

**Definition**: someone who behaves like a stereotypical straight man and has low emotional intelligence, especially regarding feminine topics

**Notes**: 直男 (zhí nán) = straight man

## zhinu, niece, straight girl (侄女, 直女)

**Definition**: heterosexual woman

**Notes**: 侄女 (zhínǚ) = niece, which sounds like 直女 (zhínǚ) = straight girl

## zhongtai, zt, sino-thai, chinese and thai (中泰), linhexi (林和西)

**Definition**: GNZ48, GNZ48's Star Dream Theater

**Notes**: GNZ48's theater is located in Zhongtai International Plaza (中泰国际广场) on Linhe West Road. 林和西 (lín hé xī) = Linhe West

## zhongxin, center (中心), life center (生活中心)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group dormitories

**Notes**: 生活中心 (shēnghuó zhōngxīn) = living center

## zhouma (周马), zhou🐴 (周 🐴), zhouzong, mr. zhou (周总), bald head (光头)

**Definition**: Zhou Ma (周马), the general manager of GNZ48

**Notes**: 周总 (zhōu zǒng) = Mr. Zhou, Boss Zhou

## zhwm

**Definition**: zhwm

**Notes**: 震撼我妈 (zhèn hàn wǒ mā) = shocked my mom

## zici

**Definition**: fan support, to support

**Notes**: from 支持 (zhīchí) = support

## zijie (子杰), wzj, wangzong (王总), laowang, pharaoh (老王), his royal highness zijie (子杰殿下), ceo wang

**Definition**: Wang Zijie (王子杰), the president of Siba Media and producer of SNH48 Group

**Notes**: 子杰殿下 (zijié diànxià) = His Highness Zijie

## zipai (自拍)

**Definition**: selfie

## zql

## zr, ps

**Definition**: plastic surgery, cosmetic surgery

## zzzq
