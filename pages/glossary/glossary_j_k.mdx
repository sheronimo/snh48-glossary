# Glossary: J - K

## jdl

**Definition**: reasonable, talking sense

**Notes**: 讲道理 (jiǎng dàolǐ) = reasonable

## ji (姬)

**Definition**: lesbian, gay girl

**Notes**: 姬 (jī) sounds like 基 (jī), slang meaning "gay"

## ji lian zhi (姬恋直)

**Definition**: gay girl in love with a straight girl

**Notes**: 姬恋直 (jī liàn zhí) = "gay loves straight"

## jiang, sauce (酱), -chan

**Definition**: suffix used after a person's name (usually a young woman, child, or close friend) to sound cute or endearing

**Notes**: from the Japanese ちゃん (-chan), a diminuitive honorific

## jiaozuoren (教做人), jzr

**Definition**: intrusively telling someone how to live their life or properly conduct themselves

**Notes**: 教做人 (jiào zuò rén) = "teach you how to be a person"

## jiaxing road (嘉兴路), jxl, headquarters (本部)

**Definition**: SNH48, SNH48's Star Dream Theater

**Notes**: SNH48's theater is located on Jiaxing Road, 嘉兴路 (jiā xìng lù)

## jiayou (加油), ⛽️

**Definition**: go for it, come on (expression of support or encouragement)

**Notes**: 加油 (jiāyóu) = add fuel

## jida, jidar (姬达)

**Definition**: gaydar (concerning lesbians), lesdar, the ability to percieve whether someone is lesbian

**Notes**: from 姬 (jī) = lesbian, and 雷达 (léidá) = radar

## jiemei (姐妹), jm, sisters (姐妹们), jms

**Definition**: sisters, girls, ladies

**Notes**: 姐妹 (jiěmèi), 姐妹们 (jiěmèimen) = older and younger sisters

## jingua, gourd, golden gua, golden melon (金瓜)

**Definition**: someone who is renowned or outstanding when dressing in a masculine style

**Notes**: 金花 (jīn huā) = golden flower

## jinhua, golden hua, golden flower (金花)

**Definition**: someone who is renowned or outstanding when dressing in a feminine style

**Notes**: 金瓜 (jīn guā) = golden melon

## jiquan (姬圈)

**Definition**: lesbian community, lesbian circles

## jiulouyu, nine leaky fish (九漏鱼)

**Definition**: someone who has not completed China's nine-year compulsory education policy, someone who has poor writing or grammar skills or seems uneducated

**Notes**: from 九年义务教育漏网之鱼 (jiǔ nián yìwù jiàoyù lòuwǎng zhī yú) = a fish slipping through the net of the nine-year compulsory education

## jizi (集资), jz, 🍊

**Definition**: fundraising

**Notes**: 集资 (jí zī) = fundraising

## jk uniform (jk 制服), jk

**Definition**: Japanese high school girls’ school uniforms

## juju (聚聚, 菊苣, 巨巨), gatherings (聚聚)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group fans

## jzb48, jzb, dumplings list (饺子榜)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group unofficial fundraising tracker

**Notes**: 饺子榜 (jiǎo zi bǎng) = dumpling list

## k, -k

**Definition**: 1,000, multiples of 1,000 (example: 5k = 5,000)

**Notes**: from "kilo"

## k2, seishun girls (青春派对)

**Definition**: 青春派对 ("Young Girls"), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team K's 2nd stage "Seishun Girls"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team K 2nd Stage"

## k4, saishuu bell ga naru (最后的钟声响起)

**Definition**: 最后的钟声响起 ("The Last Bell Rings"), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team K's 4th stage "Saishuu Bell ga Naru"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team K 4th Stage"

## k5, saka agari (逆流而上)

**Definition**: 逆流而上 ("Swimming Upstream"), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team K's 5th stage "Saka Agari"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team K 5th Stage"

## k6, reset (勇气重生)

**Definition**: 勇气重生 ("Courage Reborn"), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team K's 6th stage "RESET"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team K 6th Stage"

## kawei, coffee rank, coffee position (咖位)

**Definition**: a celebrity's status or rank within the entertainment industry

**Notes**: 咖位 (kā wèi) = coffee position

## kazakh, kazakhstan (哈人)

**Definition**: scary

**Notes**: 哈人 (hā rén) sounds like 吓人 (xiàrén) = scary, creepy

## kdfj, pocket room (口袋房间)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group members’ personal chat rooms in Pocket48

**Notes**: 口袋房间 (kǒu dài fáng jiān) = pocket room

## kdl

**Definition**: “my ship is sailing!”, “I ship it!”. used to express excitement over a CP

**Notes**: 磕到了 (kē dào le) = knocked

## ke gua ke hua (可瓜可花)

**Definition**: good at being both gua and hua styles

**Notes**: 可瓜可花 (kě guā kě huā) = can gua, can hua

## keyboard man, keyboard warrior (键盘侠)

**Definition**: someone who acts tough online but is timid in real life

**Notes**: 键盘侠 (jiànpán xiá) = keyboard man. also 鍵盤俠

## knock (磕), knock cp (磕 cp), nibble (嗑), nibble cp (嗑 cp)

**Definition**: "shipping" a CP, taking a strong interest in a CP and their interactions

**Notes**: 磕 (kē) = to knock. also 嗑 (kē) = to nibble (as in, shipping a CP = eating the candy of a CP's interactions)

## kol

**Definition**: social media influencer, authoritative figure in a specific field

**Notes**: from "key opinion leader"

## kongping, kp, comment control, control review (控评), empty bottle (空瓶)

**Definition**: fans manipulating a comment section's algorithm to only display positive comments about their idol

**Notes**: 控评 (kòngpíng) = comment control

## kongtai (控台)

**Definition**: control panel operator

**Notes**: from 控制台 (kòngzhì tái) = console, control desk

## kpi

**Definition**: required tasks SNH48 members must complete each week, such as posting on social media or livestreaming

**Notes**: from "key performance indicators"

## kswl

**Definition**: “I’m dying from shipping!". used to express being overwhelmed with excitement over a CP’s interaction

**Notes**: 磕死我了 (kē sǐ wǒ le) = knocked me to death

## ktv

**Definition**: karaoke, karaoke bar

**Notes**: from "karaoke television"

## kuaishou (快手), ks

**Definition**: Kuaishou (快手), a Chinese short-form video platform

## ky

**Definition**: can't read the room, creating an awkward atmosphere

**Notes**: from the Japanese saying 空気読めない (kuuki yomenai) = "can't read the air"

## kyj, drug chicken, drug-addicted chicken (嗑药鸡)

**Definition**: a CP fan who indiscriminately ships CP

**Notes**: 嗑药鸡 (kē yào jī) = CP-drugged chicken
