## ma laoshi, mls, teacher ma (马老师), mayue (马跃)

**Definition**: Ma Yue (马跃), the dance director of SNH48

**Notes**: 马老师 (mǎ lǎoshī) = Teacher Ma

## mafen, mom fan (妈粉), mom (妈), mom powder (妈粉), mama fan (妈妈粉), numb (麻麻)

**Definition**: female fans who regard themselves as their idol's mother and treat their idol as such

**Notes**: 妈粉 (mā fěn) = mom fan

## maggot (蛆)

**Definition**: disgusting person

**Notes**: 蛆 (qū) = maggot

## mai cp (卖 cp, 麦 cp), sell CP (卖 cp), selling (卖), wheat (麦), mai (卖, 麦)

**Definition**: two people generating traffic from publicizing their interactions together, often in a way that makes it appear they could be in a relationship

**Notes**: 卖 CP (mài CP) = sell CP. 麦 (mài) = wheat, which sounds like 卖 (mài)

## mai ji, sell ji (卖姬), sell chicken (卖鸡), mcgee (麦基), mai card (麦咭), sell base (卖基) 麦吉

**Definition**: queerbaiting, two women acting intimate with each other for the sake of traffic

**Notes**: 卖姬 (mài jī) = "sell gay"

## mangnei, maknae (忙内)

**Definition**: the youngest member of a group

**Notes**: from the Korean 막내 (maknae) = youngest in a group

## marketing account (营销号), yxh

**Definition**: gossip accounts that spread rumors and negative news for profit

**Notes**: 营销号 (yíng xiāo hào) = marketing account

## mcn

**Definition**: brokerage company

**Notes**: from "multi-channel network"

## meal replacement (代餐), dc

**Definition**: something that resembles or is a substitute for something else

**Notes**: 代餐 (dài cān) = meal replacement

## meimei, mm, sister (妹妹)

**Definition**: younger sister, young woman

**Notes**: 妹妹 (mèimei) = younger sister, young woman

## mengbo (孟波), mengzong, mr. meng (孟总), bobo (波波)

**Definition**: Meng Bo (孟波), the general manager of CKG48

**Notes**: 孟总 (mèng zǒng) = Mr. Meng, Boss Meng

## middle two, second year (中二), secondary disease (中二病), chuuni, chuunibyou

**Definition**: delusions of grandeur in an immature sense

**Notes**: from the Japanese 中二病 (chuunibyou) = middle school second-year syndrome

## mix

**Definition**: specific types of calls performed at certain points in a song

**Notes**: the most famous mix is "Tiger, Fire, Cyber, Fiber, Diver, Viber, Jya Jya!"

## mzhy

**Definition**: full of lies

**Notes**: 满嘴谎言 (mǎnzuǐ huǎngyán) = mouth full of lies

## n dog, n gou (n 狗), engou (恩狗)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team NII fans

**Notes**: n 狗 (n gǒu), 恩狗 (ēn gǒu) = N dog

## nai t, milk t (奶 t)

**Definition**: butchy femme lesbian

**Notes**: 奶 (nǎi) = milk

## nanke, andrology (男科)

**Definition**: a CP that is unappealing to CP fans, usually due to a lack of chemistry or the pairing feeling ill-suited for each other

**Notes**: 男科 (nánkē) = andrology, which sounds like 难嗑 (nán kē) = difficult to knock

## nantong (男同)

**Definition**: gay man, gay guy

**Notes**: from 男同性恋 (nán tóngxìngliàn) = gay, male homosexual

## nc

**Definition**: moron, idiot

**Notes**: 脑残 (nǎo cán)= brain dead

## nc powder (nc 粉), ncf, brain powder (脑残粉)

**Definition**: fans who unreasonably defend their idol even if the idol has done something wrong

**Notes**: 脑残 (nǎo cán)= brain dead

## neiyu (内娱, 内鱼, 内 🐟), internal entertainment (内娱)

**Definition**: the domestic (mainland Chinese) entertainment industry

**Notes**: 内娱 (nèi yú) = internal entertainment. 🐟is from 鱼 (yú) = fish, which sounds similar to 娱

## neodynium boxing (钕拳), female boxing (女拳)

**Definition**: radical feminist (derogatory term)

**Notes**: 钕拳 (nǚ quán) sounds like 女拳 (nǚ quán) = female boxing

## neon (霓虹)

**Definition**: Japan

**Notes**: 霓虹 (níhóng) = neon, which sounds like "Nihon" (Japan)

## netease (网易), wyy, wy☁️

**Definition**: NetEase Cloud Music (网易云音乐), a Chinese equivalent to Soundcloud

**Notes**: 网易云 (wǎng yì yún) = NetEase Cloud

## new year’s single, new year’s list (新年单)

**Definition**: SNH48's yearly New Year's single

**Notes**: 新年单 (xīnnián dān) = New Year’s single

## next girls, ng, top48, dream group (梦想组)

**Definition**: members who ranked in elections from #33 to #48

**Notes**: 梦想组 (mèngxiǎng zǔ) = Dream Group

## nhjj

**Definition**: South Korean (female) fans

**Notes**: 南韩姐姐 (nánhán jiějiě) = South Korean sisters, South Korean women

## nisu, clay sculpture (泥塑)

**Definition**: female fans fantasizing about male celebrities being female or having feminine attributes (can also refer to female celebrities being fantasized about as men)

**Notes**: 泥塑 nísù = clay sculpture

## niubi (牛逼), niu b (牛 b), nb, beef beer, 🐂🍺

**Definition**: "fucking awesome", "fucking amazing"

**Notes**: 牛逼 (niú bī) = fucking awesome

## nmd

**Definition**: "your mother!" (insult)

**Notes**: 你妈的 (nǐ mā de) = your mother

## nmsl

**Definition**: "your mother is dead", "fuck your mother"

## no one cares (没人在意), nbcs

**Definition**: nobody cares

**Notes**: from "nobody cares"

## npy, bf

**Definition**: boyfriend

**Notes**: 男朋友 (nán péngyǒu) = boyfriend

## npy, gf

**Definition**: girlfriend

**Notes**: 女朋友 (nǚ péngyǒu) = girlfriend

## nsdd

**Definition**: "you're right"

**Notes**: 你说得对 (nǐ shuō dé duì) = you are correct

## nt, cerebral palsy (脑瘫)

**Definition**: moron, retard, acting stupidly (derogatory term)

**Notes**: 脑瘫 (nǎo tān) = cerebral palsy

## ntxl, neodymium copper (钕铜), neodymium (釹同, 钕瞳), female copper, female ketone (女酮), female concrete (女砼), tong tong (铜铜), copper (铜) 女峒

**Definition**: lesbian

**Notes**: from 女同性恋 (nǚ tóngxìngliàn) = lesbian, female homosexual
