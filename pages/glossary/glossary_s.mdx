# Glossary: S

## s meow (s 喵), ace meow (艾斯喵)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team SII fans

**Notes**: s 喵 (s miāo), 艾斯喵 (àisī miāo) = S meow

## s1

**Definition**: S1 无限奇迹应援会儿, a popular SNH48 Group gossip account on Weibo

**Notes**: @S1 无限奇迹应援会儿 on Weibo

## s2, te wo tsunaginagara, te wo tsuna (手牵手)

**Definition**: 手牵手 (“Hand in Hand”), a SNH48 stage adapted from SKE48 Team S's 2nd stage "Te wo Tsunaginagara"

**Notes**: from "SKE48 Team S 2nd Stage"

## sajiao (撒娇)

**Definition**: acting cute, coquettishly, or like a spoiled child. equivalent to "aegyo" in Korean or "burikko" in Japanese

**Notes**: 撒娇 (sājiāo) = coquettish

## same push (同推), oshi kaburi

**Definition**: fans who support the same idol, or whose support overlaps with each other’s

**Notes**: 同推 (tóng tuī) = same push

## sand sculpture, shadiao (沙雕)

**Definition**: acting foolish or silly

**Notes**: 沙雕 (shādiāo) = sand sculpture, which sounds like 傻屌 (shǎdiǎo) = idiot, dickhead

## sang (桑), -sang (桑), -san

**Definition**: suffix used after a person's name to show politeness

**Notes**: from the Japanese -さん (-san), an honorific similar to "Mr." or "Ms."

## sanQ (三 q), sankyu, 3q

**Definition**: thank you

**Notes**: 三 q (sān Q) sounds like "thank you". similar to the Japanese サンキュー (sankyuu) = thank you

## sao (骚)

**Definition**: sexy or flirty, but in a campy manner

**Notes**: 骚 (sāo) = coquettish

## screen party (屏幕党), online party (线上党)

**Definition**: fans who watch theater shows and events remotely

**Notes**: 屏幕党 (píngmù dǎng) = screen party, screen community member

## sea king (海王)

**Definition**: womanizer, player

**Notes**: 海王 (hǎiwáng) = sea king. (if women are like fish in the sea, then a "sea king" is someone "fishing" for several women at once)

## sea view (海景), sea view room (海景房)

**Definition**: a fan running away with money fundraised for an idol, a fan club embezzling funds

**Notes**: 海景房 (hǎijǐng fáng) = sea view room (if a fan runs away with other fans' money, they'll be rich enough to buy a house with a view of the sea)

## seine river (塞纳河), seine (塞纳), the river, he (河)

**Definition**: SNH48, SNH48 Group

**Notes**: 塞纳河 (sāinà hé) = Seine River, which has the initials "SNH"

## selection (选拔), senbatsu, top16, top girls, starlight group (星光组)

**Definition**: the lineup of members selected for a single or other activity, members who ranked in elections from #1 to #16

**Notes**: 星光组 (xīngguāng zǔ) = Starlight Group

## sell ji (直女卖姬，天打雷劈)

**Definition**: "straight girls who queerbait will be struck by lightning"

## sell miserable (卖惨)

**Definition**: showing off one's misery in order to elicit sympathy or attention

**Notes**: 卖惨 (mài cǎn) = selling misery, selling a sob story

## seno, senno, seeno, se-no

**Definition**: a chant used when preparing to start something

**Notes**: from the Japanese せーの (seeno) = "ready, go!", "one, two, three, go!"

## shenba (沈芭), sb, shenyang branch (沈阳分团)

**Definition**: SHY48 management

**Notes**: 沈阳丝芭文化传媒有限公司 = Shenyang Siba Culture Media Co., Ltd.

## shenqi (神七), kami7, top7

**Definition**: the 7 highest-ranking or most popular members of a 48 group

**Notes**: from the Japanese 神 7 (kami 7) = god seven

## shentui, god push (神推), kami-oshi, kami

**Definition**: a fan's absolute favorite idol

**Notes**: from the Japanese 神推し (kami-oshi) = god-oshi

## shenyu (沈渝)

**Definition**: SHY48 and CKG48

**Notes**: from "Shenyang" and 渝 (yú) = Chongqing

## shi lezhi (石乐志)

**Definition**: "losing my mind"

**Notes**: 石乐志 (shí lè zhì) sounds like 失了智 (shīle zhì) = lost my mind

## shijian, view rape (视奸), sj

**Definition**: stalking, silently searching through, or keeping tabs on someone's social media

**Notes**: 视奸 (shì jiān) = stare rape, to sexually harass someone through one's gaze

## shiqinghuayi, poetry and painting (诗情画奕), sqhy, 4781, yutian (宇甜), yizhou (奕粥, 野猪), yz, 🐗

**Definition**: SQHY (诗情画奕), the CP of Zhou Shiyu and Wang Yi

**Notes**: 4781 (sì qī bā yī) sounds like 诗情画奕 (shī qíng huà yì)

## shonichi, first day (初日)

**Definition**: the first performance of a theater show or stage play

**Notes**: from the Japanese 初日 (shonichi) = opening day

## shuangtui, st, double push (双推), double burden (双担)

**Definition**: a fan who supports two idols at once

**Notes**: 双推 (shuāng tuī) = double push

## shy48, shy, social shake (社会摇), shihuiyao (石灰窑)

**Definition**: SHY48, a Chinese female idol group

**Notes**: from "Shenyang 48"

## siba (丝芭), shangba (上芭), sb, ba (芭), star48, 48 (siba)

**Definition**: Siba Media (丝芭传媒), SNH48 management, SNH48 Group management

**Notes**: 上海丝芭文化传媒集团有限公司 = Shanghai Star48 Culture Media Group Co., Ltd. (Siba Media)

## single arrow (单箭头)

**Definition**: one person having a crush on or unrequited love for another person

**Notes**: 单箭头 (dān jiàntóu) = single-headed arrow

## single dog (单身 ​ 狗)

**Definition**: lonely person with no boyfriend or girlfriend

**Notes**: 单身狗 (dānshēn​gǒu) = single dog

## sisheng (私生), ss, sisheng fans, ssf, illegitimate meals (私生饭), sasaeng

**Definition**: obsessive fans who stalk idols and invade their private lives

**Notes**: 私生饭 (sīshēng fàn) = illegitimate fan

## sister group (姊妹團体), branch group (分团)

**Definition**: a regional 48 group based in a specific city or area

**Notes**: 姊妹團体 (zǐmèi tuántǐ) = sister group

## sjb

**Definition**: insane, out of your mind

**Notes**: 神经病 (shénjīngbìng) = neuropathy

## sjd

**Definition**: [CP] is fake (is not a real romantic relationship)

**Notes**: 是假的 (shì jiǎ de) = it is fake

## sl, private association (私联), private contact (私联)

**Definition**: private communication between fans and idols that is conducted outside of official work channels

**Notes**: 私联 (sī lián) = private association

## slag, scum (渣)

**Definition**: scumbag, someone who plays with women's feelings

**Notes**: 渣 (zhā) = slag

## slice (切片)

**Definition**: cuts or short clips of a livestream

**Notes**: 切片 (qiēpiàn) = slice

## small fans, little fans, xiao fensi (小粉丝), xfs

**Definition**: regular fans, ordinary fans

**Notes**: 小粉丝 (xiǎo fěnsī) = small fans, little fans

## sn, sh, sx, nh, nx, hx

**Definition**: any two SNH48 teams being referred to together (example: "sn" = Team SII and Team NII)

**Notes**: from "Team SII, Team NII, Team HII, Team X"

## snh48 group, snh group, snhg, snh48 family, china48, chn48

**Definition**: SNH48 and its sister groups as a whole

**Notes**: refers to SNH48 and all related groups

## snh48, snh

**Definition**: SNH48, a Chinese female idol group based in Shanghai

**Notes**: from "Shanghai 48"

## soy sauce (打酱油)

**Definition**: "none of my business"

**Notes**: 打酱油 (dǎ jiàngyóu) = to buy soy sauce (as in, "it's none of my business. i'm just here to buy soy sauce")

## spray (喷)

**Definition**: to sharply scold or criticize

**Notes**: 喷 (pēn) = spray

## squat (蹲, 蹲个)

**Definition**: waiting for additional information or other people's comments

**Notes**: 蹲 (dūn) = squat

## srds

**Definition**: "even though"

**Notes**: 虽然但是 (suī rán dàn shì) = even though

## stalk, stem (梗)

**Definition**: meme

**Notes**: 梗 (gěng) = stalk, stem. from 哏 (gén) = funny

## star chasing (追星)

**Definition**: being a fan of a celebrity

**Notes**: 追星 (zhuī xīng) = chase stars

## star dream theater, xingmeng theater (星梦剧院), xingmeng (星梦)

**Definition**: SNH48's theater, SNH48 Group’s regional theaters

**Notes**: 星梦 (xīng mèng) = star dream

## stf, scholar-official (士大夫)

**Definition**: staff member

**Notes**: from "staff"

## suan, sour (酸), lemon, 🍋

**Definition**: jealous

**Notes**: 酸 (suān) = sour

## substitute (代役), acting member, support member (代役成员)

**Definition**: a SNH48 Group member performing with a team they are not part of, usually to build experience with the team or to compensate for missing members

**Notes**: 代役成员 (dàiyì chéngyuán) = substitute member

## summer (夏天)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group general election, the general election voting and campaigning period

**Notes**: 夏天 (xiàtiān) = summer

## summer is coming (夏天快到了)

**Definition**: "election season is soon approaching"

**Notes**: 夏天快到了 (xiàtiān kuài dàole) = summer is coming

## support, cheer (應援), ouen

**Definition**: supporting an idol by cheering or calling for them

**Notes**: from the Japanese 応援/應援 (ouen) = support, cheer, aid

## support club (应援会), yyh

**Definition**: organized fan club

**Notes**: 应援会 (yìngyuán huì) = support club

## support group (应援团), yyt

**Definition**: organized fan club

**Notes**: 应援团 (yìngyuán tuán) = support group

## switch push (转推), change push (改推), retweet (转推), oshihen

**Definition**: the act of changing who one’s favorite idol is

**Notes**: from the Japanese 推し変 (oshihen) = to change oshi

## szd

**Definition**: [CP] is real (is a real romantic relationship)

**Notes**: 是真的 (shì zhēn de) = it is real
