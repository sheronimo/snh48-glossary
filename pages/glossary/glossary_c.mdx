# Glossary: C

<Callout type='info'>
	Copy the link to the definition by right-clicking/long-tapping the term
	heading and choosing 'Copy Link' from the context menu.
</Callout>

## c position (c 位), c, center

**Definition**: the idol who takes the center position on stage or in group promotions

**Notes**: c 位 (c wèi) = center position

## c zhan, c site, station c (c 站), cctv

**Definition**: CCTV (China Central Television), China's national television broadcaster

**Notes**: c 站 (c zhàn) = website C

## cake, pie (饼)

**Definition**: high-profile outside jobs, activities, or resources, such as filming for a movie

**Notes**: 饼 (bǐng) = cake

## call, chant

**Definition**: synchronized audience chanting during an idol performance

**Notes**: from "call"

## candy, sugar (糖), 🍬

**Definition**: a CP's sweet interactions

**Notes**: 糖 (táng) = candy, sugar

## captain, team leader (队长)

**Definition**: a 48 Group member that leads and takes responsibility for their team

**Notes**: 队长 (duìzhǎng) = captain, team leader

## car (车), 🚗

**Definition**: smut, sexual content warning

**Notes**: used in fan fiction. any car emoji can be used

## career fan (事业粉)

**Definition**: fans who focus on supporting their idol's career development

**Notes**: 事业粉 (shìyè fěn) = career fan

## cat48, dog48, pet48

**Definition**: SNH48 Group members' pets (the majority of them are cats)

**Notes**: from "cat" or "pet" and "SNH48"

## cb brain (cp 脑), cpn

**Definition**: seeing CP content where there is none, trying to make unrelated events about a CP

**Notes**: cp 脑 (cp nǎo) = CP brain

## cbyl

**Definition**: shameless

**Notes**: 凑不要脸 (còu bùyào liǎn) = shameless

## cgt48, cgt, xijiti (稀鸡踢), xijijte (西吉特)

**Definition**: CGT48, a sister group of SNH48 based in Chengdu

**Notes**: from "Chengdu 48" (Chengtu 48). 稀鸡踢 (xī jī tī) and 西吉特 (xī jí tè) sound like "CGT"

## chaohua, ch, supertopic (超话), st

**Definition**: Weibo Super Topic, community pages on Weibo for specific interests

**Notes**: 超话 (chāo huà) = super topic

## chengba (成芭), cb, chengdu branch (成都分团)

**Definition**: CGT48 management

**Notes**: 成都丝芭文化传媒有限公司 = Chengdu Siba Culture Media Co., Ltd.

## chess king (棋王), little chess king (小棋王)

**Definition**: someone who "plays chess" by subtly manipulating people or situations to their advantage

**Notes**: 小棋王 (xiǎo qíwáng) = little chess king

## chicken legs, drumsticks (鸡腿), 🍗

**Definition**: the currency used in Pocket48 on idol gifts and services

## chigua, eat melon (吃瓜)

**Definition**: reading or discussing rumors

**Notes**: originally refers to netizens who don't know the truth about an incident and just "sit in the front row while eating melons"

## chongba (重芭), cb, chongqing branch (重庆分团)

**Definition**: CKG48 management

**Notes**: 重庆丝芭文化传媒有限公司 = Chongqing Siba Culture Media Co., Ltd.

## ckg48, ckg, xikaiji (西开姬), length width height (长宽高)

**Definition**: CKG48, a sister group of SNH48 based in Chongqing

**Notes**: from "Chonqing 48" (Chungking 48)

## clear votes (明票)

**Definition**: election votes that are casted publicly

**Notes**: 明票 (míng piào) = clear votes

## cold knowledge (冷知識)

**Definition**: trivia that is not well-known, simple facts that have been overlooked by others

**Notes**: 冷知識 (lěng zhīshì) = cold knowledge

## cold violence (冷暴力)

**Definition**: a type of emotional abuse characterized by indifference or contempt towards another person

**Notes**: 冷暴力 (lěng bàolì) = cold violence

## concurrency (兼任), kennin

**Definition**: having a position in two different 48 Groups or teams at once

**Notes**: from the Japanese 兼任 (kennin) = holding two positions concurrently

## confused, muddled, vague (糊)

**Definition**: flopping, being unpopular

**Notes**: 糊 (hú) = muddled

## cos, cosplay

**Definition**: dressing up as a particular character

**Notes**: from the Japanese コスプレ (kosupure) = costume play

## coser

**Definition**: cosplayer, someone who does cosplay

**Notes**: from "cosplayer"

## cosplay circle, cos circle (cos 圈)

**Definition**: cosplay fandom, the cosplay community

**Notes**: cos 圈 (cos quān) = cosplay circle

## counterattack (逆袭)

**Definition**: an underdog rising up

**Notes**: 逆袭 (nì xí) = counterattack

## cousin (堂哥), second cousin (二堂姐)

**Definition**: Li Yitong

**Notes**: from 明星殿堂二姐 (míngxīng diàntáng èr jiě) = Star Palace second sister

## cover the sky with one hand (一手遮天)

**Definition**: to decieve the public and hide facts or truth

**Notes**: 一手遮天 (yī shǒu zhē tiān) = to block out the sky with one hand and convince others that it does not exist

## cowherd (牛郎)

**Definition**: gigolo, male escort, host

**Notes**: 牛郎 (niúláng) = cowherd. the term's alternate meaning comes from the movie Midnight Cowboy

## cp gay, cpgay, cpg, -gay, -g

**Definition**: fan of a SNH48 Group CP

**Notes**: -gay is used as a suffix. example: KaHuang CP fans can be referred to as "kahuang gay" or "khg"

## cp, sipi (西皮), xipi (稀丕), pairing, ship

**Definition**: two people paired or “shipped” together

**Notes**: from "character pairing", "couple pairing", or "coupling"

## cpfan, cp fan, cpf, cp powder (cp 粉)

**Definition**: fan of a CP, shipper

**Notes**: from "CP fan"

## cpyyh

**Definition**: organized fan club dedicated to a CP

**Notes**: from "CP" and 应援会 (yìngyuán huì) = support club

## cue, Q

**Definition**: to transfer a conversation to someone or bring attention to them

**Notes**: from "cue"

## cultivation (养成), cultivation system (养成系)

**Definition**: fans forming a bond with an idol by following the growth of the idol's popularity, skills, and personal development

**Notes**: 养成系 (yǎngchéng xì) = cultivation system

## cut leeks (割韭菜)

**Definition**: to play someone for a fool, to scam someone of their money

**Notes**: 割韭菜 (gē jiǔcài) = cutting leeks

## cx

**Definition**: plagiarism. also used to indicate when a post or topic was inspired by another post

**Notes**: 抄袭 (chāoxí) = plagiarism

## cylx

**Definition**: a garbage or worthless group of people

**Notes**: 臭鱼烂虾 (chòu yú làn xiā) = smelly fish and rotten shrimp
