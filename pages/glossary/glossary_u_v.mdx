# Glossary: U - V

## u1s1, y1s1

**Definition**: to tell you the truth, speaking honestly

**Notes**: 有一说一 (yǒu yī shuō yī) = to speak the truth

## under girls, ug, top32, goofy group (高飞组)

**Definition**: members who ranked in elections from #17 to #32

**Notes**: 高飞组 (gāo fēi zǔ) = Fly High Group

## underground idol (地下偶像), earth puppet, diou (地偶), indie idol, chika idol, live idol

**Definition**: idols that do not appear in mass media and are not signed with a company

**Notes**: from the Japanese 地下アイドル (chika aidoru) = underground idol

## unspeakable (不可说), unspeakable CP (不可说 CP), bks

**Definition**: famous SNH48 Group CPs that ended on infamously bad terms

**Notes**: 不可说 (bù kě shuō) = unspeakable

## unspeakable 1.0, bks 1.0 (不可说 1.0)

**Definition**: Kahuang (卡黄), the CP of Li Yitong and Huang Tingting

**Notes**: 不可说 1.0 (bù kě shuō 1.0) = unspeakable #1

## unspeakable 2.0, bks 2.0 (不可说 2.0)

**Definition**: Heimiao (黑喵), the CP of Yuan Yiqi and Shen Mengyao

**Notes**: 不可说 2.0 (bù kě shuō 2.0) = unspeakable #2

## unspeakable 3.0, bks 3.0 (不可说 3.0)

**Definition**: Zuojia (左佳), the CP of Zuo Jingyuan and Tang Lijia

**Notes**: 不可说 3.0 (bù kě shuō 3.0) = unspeakable #3

## unspeakable 4.0, bks 4.0 (不可说 4.0)

**Definition**: Malu (马鹿), the CP of Feng Xinduo and Lu Ting

**Notes**: 不可说 4.0 (bù kě shuō 4.0) = unspeakable #4

## unspeakable 5.0, bks 5.0 (不可说 5.0)

**Definition**: Daimo (戴莫), the CP of Dai Meng and Mo Han. sometimes refers to Aizhu (艾朱), the CP of Zeng Aijia and Zhu Yixin

**Notes**: 不可说 5.0 (bù kě shuō 5.0) = unspeakable #5

## up master (up 主), up pig (up 猪)

**Definition**: uploader, content creator

**Notes**: up 主 (up zhǔ) = upload master

## uu, uus, uumen (uu 们)

**Definition**: friends (friendly way to refer to other netizens)

**Notes**: 友友们 (yǒu yǒu men) = friends

## versailles (凡尔), versailles literature (凡尔赛文学)

**Definition**: flaunting one's wealth or status in a way that comes off as patronizing

**Notes**: 凡尔赛文学 (fán'ěrsài wénxué) = Versailles literature. from the manga series The Rose of Versailles

## vg

**Definition**: Victoria.G, a GNZ48 stage

**Notes**: from "Victoria.G"

## vice captain, deputy captain (副队长)

**Definition**: a 48 Group member that assists the team captain in their responsibilities

**Notes**: 副队长 (fù duìzhǎng) = vice captain, deputy captain
