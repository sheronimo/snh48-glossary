# Glossary: L

## l zhan, l site, station l (l 站), lehu (乐乎), lofter

**Definition**: LOFTER (乐乎), a Chinese blogging platform

**Notes**: l 站 (l zhàn) = website L

## lala (拉拉), les, le

**Definition**: lesbian

**Notes**: 拉拉 (lālā) = hold hands

## lalang (拉郎)

**Definition**: to ship two unrelated or unwilling people together

**Notes**: from the Chinese folk saying 拉郎配 (lā láng pèi), which refers to elders forcing arranged marriage on unwilling youth with no feelings for each other

## landlord, host (楼主), lz, op

**Definition**: Internet post's owner, Internet forum thread's original poster

**Notes**: 楼主 (lóuzhǔ) = landlord

## lang, wave (浪), 🌊, dd, md

**Definition**: someone who is a fan of many idols at once (often a demeaning term)

**Notes**: 浪 (làng) = wave

## lao caipi, old cabbage (老菜皮)

**Definition**: worthless old woman

**Notes**: 老菜皮 (lǎo cài pí) = old vegetable skin

## laogong (老公), lg

**Definition**: husband, hubby (term of endearment)

**Notes**: 老公 (lǎogōng) = husband

## laopo (老婆), lp

**Definition**: wife, wifey (term of endearment)

**Notes**: 老婆 (lǎopó) = wife

## lapiao, canvassing (拉票)

**Definition**: special individual performances held during the SNH48 Group election voting period

**Notes**: 拉票 (lāpiào) = to canvass or campaign for votes

## lbs48, lbs, green account

**Definition**: LBS48, a popular SNH48 Group news and gossip account on Weibo

**Notes**: LBS is derived from 路边社 (lù biān shè) = anecdotal or unsourced news

## led

**Definition**: GNZ48's live performance control operators (responsible for lighting, music, timing, etc.)

**Notes**: from "LED" (light-emitting diode)

## li flag (立 flag, 立 🚩)

**Definition**: to set a goal, to fulfill the conditions needed for an event to occur

**Notes**: 立 flag (lì flag) = establish a flag, set up a flag

## li tao (李涛)

**Definition**: rational discussion

**Notes**: from 理性讨论 (lǐxìng tǎolùn) = rational discussion

## lianmai, even wheat, mic connect (连麦)

**Definition**: digital broadcast where people in different locations connect and stream together

## lick (舔), dog licking (舔狗)

**Definition**: excessively flattering other people or deprecating oneself, acting sycophantically

**Notes**: 舔狗 (tiǎn gǒu) = lick dog

## literature (文学), humanities (同人文)

**Definition**: fan fiction

**Notes**: 文学 (wénxué) = literature, humanities

## little idol (小偶像), xox

**Definition**: SNH48 Group members, or any young and inexperienced idol

**Notes**: 小偶像 (xiǎo ǒuxiàng) = little idol

## liuliang (流量), traffic star (流量明星)

**Definition**: a celebrity whose popularity is characterized by a massive and dedicated online fanbase or strong social media metrics

**Notes**: 流量明星 (liúliàng míngxīng) = traffic star

## lllllb

**Definition**: lagging behind, out of touch

**Notes**: from "lay back"

## lo niang (lo 娘)

**Definition**: a woman who frequently wears Lolita fashion

**Notes**: from "Lolita" and 娘 (niáng) = woman, young lady

## lo, lolita (洛丽塔), lolita fashion (洛丽塔风尚)

**Definition**: a fashion style and subculture characterized by elaborate Victorian-style petticoat skirts and cute aesthetics

**Notes**: from the Japanese ロリータ (roriita) = Lolita. while the term is derived from Vladimir Nabokov's novel Lolita, Lolita fashion as a style has no sexual connotations

## longhu (龙湖), yinpai power (寅派动力)

**Definition**: CKG48 (post-theater closing), CKG48's current performance venue

**Notes**: CKG48 currently performs at Chongqing Impact Motion, 寅派动力 ("Yinpai Power")

## love ban, love prohibition, dating ban, romance ban (恋爱禁止), renai kinshi jourei, renai kinshi, rkj

**Definition**: the rule forbidding idols from having romantic relationships

**Notes**: 恋爱禁止 (liànài jìnzhǐ) = love ban

## love brain (恋爱脑)

**Definition**: someone who makes bad or foolish decisions because romance and love have clouded their judgment

**Notes**: 恋爱脑 (liànài nǎo) = love brain

## lowb

**Definition**: idiot

**Notes**: from "low brain", which sounds like "no brain"

## lsp

**Definition**: pervert, someone who likes sexual content

**Notes**: 老色痞 (lǎo sè pǐ) = dirty old man

## luren (路人), passerby (路人)

**Definition**: normal person with little to no interest in idols

**Notes**: 路人 (lùrén) = passerby

## lutou (路透), reuters (路透)

**Definition**: private celebrity content (such as photos and videos) leaked through unofficial channels (such as fans or paparazzi)

**Notes**: 路透 (lùtòu) = Reuters

## luzhou (绿洲), lz, oasis (绿洲)

**Definition**: Oasis (绿洲), a Chinese equivalent to Instagram

**Notes**: 绿洲 (lǜ zhōu) = oasis
