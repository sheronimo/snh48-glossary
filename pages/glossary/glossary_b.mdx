# Glossary: B

## b zhan, b site, station b (b 站), bilibili (哔哩哔哩), bili2

**Definition**: Bilibili (哔哩哔哩), a Chinese video sharing platform

**Notes**: b 站 (b zhàn) = website B

## b3, pajama drive (不眠之夜)

**Definition**: 不眠之夜 ("Sleepless Night"), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team B's 3rd stage "Pajama Drive"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team B 3rd Stage"

## b4, idol no yoake (偶像的黎明)

**Definition**: 偶像的黎明 (“Dawn of the Idols”), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team B's 4th stage "Idol no Yoake"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team B 4th Stage"

## b5, theater no megami (剧场女神)

**Definition**: 剧场女神 (“Theater Goddess”), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team B's 5th stage "Theater no Megami"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team B 5th Stage"

## baba48 (芭芭 48), 8848

**Definition**: 芭芭 48, a popular SNH48 Group news and gossip account on Weibo

**Notes**: @芭芭 48 on Weibo

## back garden (后花园), hhy, give birth to a girl (生个女孩吧)

**Definition**: 生个女孩吧 ("Give birth to a girl bar"), an infamous SNH48 discussion forum on Baidu Tieba

**Notes**: 后花园 (hòu huā yuán) = back garden. 生个女孩吧 on Baidu Tieba

## baihe (百合), lily (百合), yuri, girls love, gl

**Definition**: media focused on lesbianism or intimate relationships between female characters

**Notes**: 百合 (bǎihé) = lily. from the Japanese 百合 (yuri) = lily

## baipiao, white prostitute (白嫖), bp, white p (白 p), baipiao fans, prostitute fans (白嫖粉)

**Definition**: fans who are unwilling to spend money on their idol but still express support for them

**Notes**: originally refers to people who visit prostitutes without paying them

## banjia, moving house (搬家)

**Definition**: an artist's fandom making a concerted effort to promote their artist from Weibo's 明星势力榜 ("Star Power Chart") to its 内地榜 ("Mainland Chart")

**Notes**: 搬家 (bānjiā) = moving house

## bashu (巴蜀)

**Definition**: the Sichuan basin area, Chongqing and Chengdu

**Notes**: from 巴 (bā) = the ancient Chinese kingdom of Ba, and 蜀 (shǔ) = the ancient Chinese kingdom of Shu, which one existed in the Sichuan basin

## bdjw

**Definition**: used when seeking an answer for a question

**Notes**: 不懂就问 (bù dǒng jiù wèn) = ask if you don't undestand

## be, cp be, cpbe

**Definition**: a CP that ended on bad terms or is no longer in a shippable state

**Notes**: from "bad ending"

## beiba (北芭), bb, beijing branch (北京分团)

**Definition**: BEJ48 management

**Notes**: 北京丝芭文化传媒有限公司 = Beijing Siba Culture Media Co., Ltd.

## beiguang (北广)

**Definition**: BEJ48 and GNZ48

**Notes**: from "Beijing" and "Guangzhou"

## bej48, bej, biyijie, otherworld (彼异界), beienjun (贝恩郡)

**Definition**: BEJ48, a sister group of SNH48 based in Beijing

**Notes**: from "Beijing 48". 彼异界 (bǐ yì jiè) and 贝恩郡 (bèi ēn jùn) sound like "BEJ"

## bent, curved (是弯)

**Definition**: gay, gay person

**Notes**: 是弯 (shì wān) = bent (as in, "not straight")

## best partners (最佳拍档), partners (拍档), bp, zjpd

**Definition**: a SNH48 Group tournament event where members form CPs and compete against each other through stage performances

**Notes**: 最佳拍档 (zuì jiā pāidàng) = best partners

## bg

**Definition**: media focused on heterosexuality or intimate relationships between male and female characters

**Notes**: from "boy X girl"

## bhys, 8hys

**Definition**: sorry, excuse me (feeling embarrassed)

**Notes**: 不好意思 (bù hǎo yì si) = sorry, excuse me

## biantai (变态)

**Definition**: pervert

**Notes**: 变态 (biàntài) = metamorphosis. also from the Japanese 変態 (hentai) = metamorphosis

## big coffee (大咖)

**Definition**: a big-name celebrity within the entertainment industry

**Notes**: 大咖 (dà kā) = big coffee

## birthday performance (生日公演), shenggong (生公), birthday stage, seitansai

**Definition**: special stage celebrating a 48 Group member's birthday

**Notes**: from the Japanese 聖誕祭 (seitansai) = birthday festival

## bixian, bx, avoid suspicion, avoid sus (避嫌)

**Definition**: two people in a CP deliberately avoiding public contact with each other

**Notes**: 避嫌 (bìxián) = avoid arousing suspicion

## bking, bk

**Definition**: someone acting cool who actually isn't cool

**Notes**: from 装逼 (zhuāng bī) = pretending to be awesome, and "king"

## bksn

**Definition**: as expected of you

**Notes**: 不愧是你

## bl

**Definition**: media focused on male homosexuality or intimate relationships between male characters

**Notes**: from "boys' love"

## black turns powder (黑转粉)

**Definition**: an anti of an idol turning into an fan of that idol

**Notes**: 黑转粉 (hēi zhuǎn fěn) = anti turned fan

## blasting (爆破)

**Definition**: exposing something, blowing something up

**Notes**: 爆破 (bàopò) = blast, blow up

## blood sugar (血糖), eating blood sugar (嗑血糖)

**Definition**: CP fans leeching off a popular member's traffic in a CP where one member has significantly more popularity than the other

**Notes**: full meaning is "eating CP candy while sucking blood (from the popular member of the CP)"

## blx

**Definition**: acting oversensitive, mentally weak, or glass-hearted

**Notes**: 玻璃心 (bō lí xīn) = glass heart

## brainwashing bag (洗脑包), xnb

**Definition**: misinformation campaign designed to control the public's opinion of someone

**Notes**: 洗脑包 (xǐ nǎo bāo) = brainwashing package

## break defense (破防)

**Definition**: to be taken aback, to have an emotional breakdown

**Notes**: 破防 (pò fáng) = break defense (as in, one's mental defenses being broken)

## bright future (前程似锦), qcsj

**Definition**: farewell blessing when an idol graduates

**Notes**: 前程似锦 (qián chéng sì jǐn) = bright future

## brush (刷), swipe (刷)

**Definition**: to browse

**Notes**: 刷 (shuā) = brush, scrub

## buddhist star chasing (佛系追星)

**Definition**: being a fan of a celebrity in a peaceful and moderate manner

**Notes**: 佛系追星 (fúxì zhuī xīng) = Buddhist star chasing

## building, floor (楼)

**Definition**: online forum thread

**Notes**: 楼 (lóu) = building, thread

## business ability (运营能力)

**Definition**: an idol's skill at interacting with fans, building their fanbase, and maintaining their idol image

**Notes**: 运营能力 (yùnyíng nénglì) = business ability

## business cp, commercial cp (商业 cp)

**Definition**: a CP formed for the purpose of making money or gaining publicity

**Notes**: 营业 (yíngyè), 商业 (shāngyè) = business
