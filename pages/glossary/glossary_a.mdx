## a zhan, a site, station a, acfun (a 站)

**Definition**: AcFun, a Chinese video sharing platform

**Notes**: a 站 (a zhàn) = website A

## a5, renai kinshi jourei (永恒之光)

**Definition**: 永恒之光 ("Eternal Light"), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team A's 5th stage "Renai Kinshi Jourei"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team A 5th Stage"

## a6, mokugekisha (前所未有)

**Definition**: 前所未有 ("Unprecedented"), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Team A's 6th stage "Mokugekisha"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team A 6th Stage"

## abb

**Definition**: one Chinese character followed by another Chinese character that is repeated twice

**Notes**: examples: Liu Qianqian (刘倩倩), Su Shanshan (苏杉杉), Huang Tingting (黄婷婷)

## abo, a/b/o, omegaverse

**Definition**: erotic fanfiction genre where humans are given wolf-like characteristics and divided into social classes: alphas (A), betas (B), and omegas (O)

**Notes**: from "alpha, beta, omega"

## abuse fans (虐粉, 虐分)

**Definition**: idols portraying themselves as miserable or disadvantaged in order to drive their fans to support them more

**Notes**: 虐粉 (nüè fěn) = abuse fans

## ace

**Definition**: the idol with the highest overall popularity or level within their group

**Notes**: from "ace"

## acg, acgn, 2d, two-dimensional (二次元)

**Definition**: Chinese otaku subculture, pertaining to fields such as anime, manga, video games, visual novels, and VTubers

**Notes**: from the Japanese 二次元 (nijigen) = second dimension. acgn = "anime, comics, games, novels"

## adqc

**Definition**: adqc

**Notes**: 爱豆青春

## after waves, after waves 9, aw, aw9, langcai girls (浪彩少女)

**Definition**: After Waves 9 (浪彩少女 AW9), a SNH48 sub-unit

## ai weiyang (爱未央), awy

**Definition**: 爱未央 (“Love Lasts Forever”), a SNH48 song

**Notes**: 爱未央 (ài wèiyāng) = love is not central

## aite (艾特), @-ing

**Definition**: tagging someone's social media username with an @

**Notes**: the symbol "@" is pronounced as 艾特 (ài tè)

## akb48 group, akbg, japan48, jpn48, 48 group, 48g, 48 series (48 系), all48

**Definition**: AKB48 and its sister groups as a whole

**Notes**: refers to AKB48 and all related groups, sometimes including SNH48 Group

## akb48 team sh, akb48sh, akbsh, team sh, tsh, sh, social team (社会队)

**Definition**: AKB48 Team SH, a Chinese female idol group

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Team Shanghai"

## akb48, akb

**Definition**: AKB48 (a Japanese female idol group originally affiliated with SNH48), AKB48 Group

**Notes**: from "Akihabara 48"

## aki-p, qiu-p (秋 P), kangzi (康子), feiqiu (肥秋)

**Definition**: Akimoto Yasushi (秋元康), the producer of AKB48 and AKB48 Group

## aks, vernalossom, verna, dh

**Definition**: Japanese 48 Group management

**Notes**: AKS Co., Ltd. was restructured into Vernalossom

## all in, allin

**Definition**: a fan club converting all of their currently available funds into votes

**Notes**: from "all in"

## aluminum copper (铝铜)

**Definition**: girl with no male friends or no romantic attention from men

**Notes**: from 铜墙铁壁女 = "iron-walled girl"

## anchor (主播)

**Definition**: livestreamer, host, presenter

**Notes**: 主播 (zhǔbō) = anchor, broadcaster

## anli, amway (安利)

**Definition**: recommendation, to recommend

**Notes**: 安利 (ānlì) = Amway

## anti (安替), anti-fan, black powder, black fan, heifen (黑粉), sunspot, heizi, hz (黑子)

**Definition**: someone who actively hates and opposes an idol or celebrity

**Notes**: 黑粉 (hēi fěn) = black fan. 黑子 (hēizǐ) = sunspots

## anti-black station (反黑站)

**Definition**: fan organizations dedicated to opposing antis and controlling public opinion about their idol

**Notes**: 反黑站 (fǎn hēi zhàn) = anti-black station

## anti-fading, anti-forgetting, prevent fading (防淡忘)

**Definition**: a post chronicling someone's past misdeeds and controversies

**Notes**: 防淡忘 (fáng dànwàng) = anti-forgetting

## archeology (考古)

**Definition**: go through old Internet posts

**Notes**: 考古 (kǎogǔ) = archeology

## awsl

**Definition**: used to express excitement, usually over something cute or charming

**Notes**: 啊我死了 (a wǒ sǐ le) = "ah, I am dead!". equivalent to "they slayed me"
