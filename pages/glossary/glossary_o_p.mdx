# Glossary: O - P

## o2o

**Definition**: a business model that uses online marketing to drive consumers to make purchases in physical stores

**Notes**: from "online to offline"

## ocq

**Definition**: SNH48 friend group consisting of Feng Xinduo, Lu Ting, Kong Xiaoyin, Qian Beiting, Sun Rui, and Xu Chenchen

**Notes**: from "old come qiao", an English translation of 老来俏

## ol

**Definition**: office lady, female office worker

**Notes**: from "office lady"

## oligei (奥利给), oligay

**Definition**: awesome, come on (expression of support or encouragement)

**Notes**: 奥利给 (àolìgěi) is an anagram of 给利奥 (gěilìào), which sounds like 给力噢 (gěilì ō) = awesome

## one space (壹空间), ekj

**Definition**: BEJ48 (after the U-Town theater closure), BEJ48's current performance venue

**Notes**: BEJ48 currently performs at THE ONE CLUB, 壹空间 (yī kōng jiān) which sounds like "ekj"

## ooc

**Definition**: a character in a fan work behaving differently from how they are in the original work or in real life

**Notes**: from "out of character"

## open close powder, open and close fans (开闭粉)

**Definition**: AKB48 fans, AKB48 Group fans

**Notes**: 开闭 (kāi bì) sounds like "AKB"

## open wheat, open microphone (开麦)

**Definition**: open mic, live singing, to begin openly communicating about something

**Notes**: from 开麦克风 (kāi màikèfēng) = turn on microphone

## opponent (对家)

**Definition**: the rival of a star or idol

**Notes**: 对家 (duì jiā) = opponent

## orange (橘)

**Definition**: lesbian

**Notes**: 橘 (jú) = mandarin orange. from the yuri manga series Citrus

## original performance (原创公演), original stage

**Definition**: theater performance with a set list of original songs

**Notes**: 原创公演 (yuánchuàng gōngyǎn) = original performance

## orthodox idol (正统偶像), orthodoxy (正统)

**Definition**: an idol who adheres to traditional Japanese idol ideals, such as being full of youthful energy and free of scandal

**Notes**: 正统偶像 (zhèngtǒng ǒuxiàng) = orthodox idol. from the Japanese 正統派アイドル (seitou-ha aidoru) = orthodox idol

## otaku (御宅族), ota

**Definition**: geek, nerd, or obsessive fan of something

**Notes**: from the Japanese おたく/ お宅 (otaku)

## out of the circle (圈外), unranked member

**Definition**: not receiving a numbered rank at the annual general elections

**Notes**: 圈外 (quān wài) = outside the circle

## out of the pit, leave the pit (出坑)

**Definition**: leaving a fandom, quitting an interest

**Notes**: 出坑 (chū kēng) = exit a pit

## ouyan (偶研), idol research project (偶像研究计划)

**Definition**: a SNH48 Group project used to build exposure and stage experience for new trainees

**Notes**: 偶像研究计划 (ǒuxiàng yánjiū jìhuà) = idol research project

## owhat, ow

**Definition**: Owhat, a fundraising website

**Notes**: from "Owhat"

## p

**Definition**: femme lesbian

**Notes**: from 婆 (pó) or "pretty girl"

## p picture (p 图), ps picture (ps 图), p

**Definition**: edited image

**Notes**: from "Photoshop" and 图片 (túpiàn) = image, picture

## p zhan, p site, station p (p 站), pixiv

**Definition**: Pixiv, a Japanese art platform

**Notes**: p 站 (zhàn) = website P

## p zhan, p site, station p (p 站), pornhub, ph

**Definition**: Pornhub, a Canadian pornography platform

**Notes**: p 站 (zhàn) = website P

## paqiang, pq, climb the wall (爬墙)

**Definition**: leaving a fandom and joining a new fandom

**Notes**: 爬墙 (pá qiáng) = wall climbing

## passerby fan (路人粉)

**Definition**: casual fan

**Notes**: 路人粉 (lùrén fěn) = passerby fan

## pheasant (野鸡), pheasant group (野鸡团)

**Definition**: AKB48 Group fans' derogatory nickname for SNH48 Group and its members

**Notes**: 野鸡 (yějī) = pheasant, but has a double meaning of "illegal/knockoff" or "prostitute"

## pick feet (抠脚)

**Definition**: someone who has nothing to do or too much free time, an idol who has a lack of scheduled work

**Notes**: 抠脚 (kōu jiǎo) = pick feet

## pink turns black (粉转黑)

**Definition**: a fan of an idol turning into an anti of that idol

**Notes**: 粉转黑 (fěn zhuǎn hēi) = fan turned anti

## pipihei, pph, wearing black skin (披皮黑)

**Definition**: antis pretending to be fans in order to sow discord or damage a fanbase's reputation

**Notes**: 披皮黑 (pī pí hēi) = wear black skin

## pk

**Definition**: a competition between two or more people (in idol fandom, often refers to a fundraising battle)

**Notes**: from "player kill"

## plato (柏拉图)

**Definition**: platonic

**Notes**: 柏拉图 (bólātú) = Plato

## pljj

**Definition**: beautiful older woman, beautiful woman

**Notes**: 漂亮姐姐 (piào liang jiě jiě) = pretty older sister

## plmm

**Definition**: beautiful younger woman, beautiful woman

**Notes**: 漂亮妹妹 (piào liang mèi mei) = pretty younger sister. also 漂亮美美

## plog

**Definition**: a social media post about one's daily life that heavily involves photos

**Notes**: from "photo vlog" or "picture vlog"

## pocket48, koudai siba (口袋 48), pocket, koudai (口袋), kd, kd48

**Definition**: Pocket48 (口袋 48), SNH48 Group’s official social media app

**Notes**: 口袋 (kǒu dài) = pocket

## poison cake (毒饼)

**Definition**: a high-profile activity that ends up damaging the artist

**Notes**: 毒饼 (dú bǐng) = poison cake

## popular science (科普), popularize (科普)

**Definition**: to explain something or help someone understand something in simple terms, to fill someone in

**Notes**: 科普 (kēpǔ) = popular science

## post-00s (00 后)

**Definition**: 2000's baby, people born between 2000 and 2009

**Notes**: 00 后 (00 hòu) = after 2000

## post-10s (10 后)

**Definition**: 2010's baby, people born between 2010 and 2019

**Notes**: 10 后 (10 hòu) = after 2010

## post-80s (80 后)

**Definition**: 80's baby, people born between 1980 and 1989

**Notes**: 80 后 (00 hòu) = after 1980

## post-90s (90 后)

**Definition**: 90's baby, people born between 1990 and 1999

**Notes**: 90 后 (00 hòu) = after 1990

## ppl

**Definition**: a romantic relationship involving two femme lesbians

**Notes**: from "P and P love"

## pua

**Definition**: emotionally manipulating someone by attacking their sense of self-worth and lowering their self-esteem

**Notes**: from "pick-up artist"

## pull flag (拔旗, 拔 🚩)

**Definition**: fulfilling a promise or completing an achievement set as a goal

**Notes**: 拔旗 (bá qí) = raise a flag, pull a flag

## pv

**Definition**: promotional video

**Notes**: sometimes used interchangeably with "MV" (music video)

## pyq

**Definition**: WeChat Moments

**Notes**: 朋友圈 (péng yǒu quān) = circle of friends
