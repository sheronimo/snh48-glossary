# Glossary: Q - R

<Callout type='info'>
	Copy the link to the definition by right-clicking/long-tapping the term
	heading and choosing 'Copy Link' from the context menu.
</Callout>

## qcyn

**Definition**: Youth With You (青春有你), a survival show series

**Notes**: 青春有你 (qīng chūn yǒu nǐ) = youth with you

## qcyn2, jing (婧) blue you 2 (青你 2)

**Definition**: Youth With You season 2 (青春有你 2)

**Notes**: 婧 (jìng) is from "女" and "青" together

## qianbei (前輩), senpai, sunbae

**Definition**: senior (in terms of age, position, or experience)

**Notes**: 前輩 (qiánbèi) = senior

## qianqiu le (千秋乐), qql, senshuuraku

**Definition**: the final performance of a theater show or stage play

**Notes**: from the Japanese 千秋楽 (senshuuraku) = final performance

## qinglang, clear (清朗), operation qinglang (清朗行动)

**Definition**: the Chinese government's crackdown on online fan culture and its elements that are deemed toxic

**Notes**: 清朗行动 (qīnglǎng xíngdòng) = Operation Clear and Bright, Operation Qinglang

## qixi (七夕)

**Definition**: Qixi Festival, also known as Chinese Valentine's Day

**Notes**: the Qixi Festival takes place on the 7th day of the 7th month of the traditional Chinese calendar

## qj

**Definition**: rape

**Notes**: 强奸 (qiángjiān) = rape

## qq

**Definition**: Tencent QQ (腾讯 QQ), a Chinese social messaging platform

## qqgg

**Definition**: weird

**Notes**: 奇奇恠恠 (qí qí guài guài) = weird

## qswl

**Definition**: qswl

**Notes**: 气死我了 (qì sǐ wǒ le) = angered to death

## quanhe, the whole river (全河)

**Definition**: all of SNH48 or SNH48 Group

**Notes**: 全河 (quán hé) = the whole river

## queen bee (蜂王)

**Definition**: Queen Bee, a Chinese chain of lesbian bars

**Notes**: 蜂王 (fēng wáng) = queen bee

## quick report (速报)

**Definition**: breaking news

**Notes**: 速报

## rainbow fart (彩虹屁), 🌈💨

**Definition**: rainbow fart (彩虹屁), 🌈💨

**Notes**: 彩虹屁 (cǎihóng pì) = rainbow fart

## ranling (冉令), rl

**Definition**: Ran Ling (冉令), the theater manager of CKG48

## red (红)

**Definition**: popular, trendy

**Notes**: 红 (hóng) = red

## rela, hot pull (热拉), thel, the l

**Definition**: Rela (热拉), a popular Chinese lesbian dating app

**Notes**: Rela is also known as The L

## remarriage (复婚), fh

**Definition**: an inactive or BE CP becoming active again

**Notes**: 复婚 (fùhūn) = remarriage

## renshen, ginseng (人参), human body (人身), rs

**Definition**: personal attack

**Notes**: 人参 (rénshēn) = ginseng. from 人身攻击 (rénshēn gōngjí) = personal attack

## rentou, head count (人头)

**Definition**: number of people (unique IDs) contributing to a fundraising PK

**Notes**: 人头 (réntóu) = number of people

## renyu, mermaid (人鱼)

**Definition**: 人鱼 ("Mermaid"), a SNH48 song

**Notes**: 人鱼 (rényú) = mermaid

## reorganization (重组) shuffle, sokaku

**Definition**: an event where many 48 Group members are simultaneously sorted into different teams

**Notes**: 重组 (chóngzǔ) = restructuring, reorganization

## repo

**Definition**: a fan's report or write-up of an idol event they attended live

**Notes**: from "report"

## request time, request hour, rh, best 50, b50, best 30, b30, golden song, golden melody, jinqu (金曲)

**Definition**: Best 50 Request Time, an annual SNH48 Group event where fans vote on which songs are performed at a concert and which members perform them

**Notes**: 金曲 (jīnqǔ) = golden song

## research student, prepatory student (预备生), kenkyuusei, kks

**Definition**: idol trainee, 48 Group trainee that acts as an understudy to members already assigned to a team

**Notes**: from the Japanese 研究生 (kenkyuusei) = research student

## resources (资源)

**Definition**: work opportunities, such as media appearances or acting roles

**Notes**: 资源 (zīyuán) = resources

## rhythm (节奏), bring rhythm (带节奏), move rhythm (动节奏)

**Definition**: to incite drama, to direct public opinion in a way that harms others or achieves one's personal goals

**Notes**: 节奏 (jiézòu) = rhythm, tempo

## rice, meal (饭), powder (粉), vermicelli, fensi (粉丝), fs

**Definition**: fans

**Notes**: 饭 (fàn) and 粉 (fěn) sound like "fan" in English. 粉丝 (fěnsī) sounds like “fans”

## river yxh (河 yxh), yxh48

**Definition**: unofficial SNH48 Group gossip, news, and update accounts on Weibo

**Notes**: from 营销号 (yíng xiāo hào) = marketing account

## rot (腐), rotten girl (腐女), fujoshi, fujo

**Definition**: women who enjoy media or CPs focused on intimate relationships between men

**Notes**: 腐女 (fǔ nǚ) = rotten girl. from the Japanese 腐女子 (fujoshi) = rotten girl

## royal, royal family (皇族)

**Definition**: an idol who is favored by management or receives resources beyond their rank

**Notes**: 皇族 (huángzú) = royal

## rps

**Definition**: shipping or consuming CP content of two real-life people

**Notes**: from "real person slash" or "real person shipping"

## rs

**Definition**: Weibo Hot Search

**Notes**: 热搜 (rè sōu) = hot search

## rub (蹭), rubbing heat (蹭热度)

**Definition**: freeloading, leeching off someone else's popularity

**Notes**: 蹭热度 (cèng rèdù) = rubbing heat
