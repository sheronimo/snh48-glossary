# Glossary: H

## h

**Definition**: switch lesbian

**Notes**: from "half"

## h dolphin, h tun (h 豚), love dolphin (爱吃豚), battleship fans (战舰粉)

**Definition**: SNH48 Team HII fans

**Notes**: h 豚 (h tún) = H dolphin. 爱吃豚 (ài chī tún) sounds like "H dolphin"

## h1, boku no taiyou (我的太阳)

**Definition**: 飓风来袭 ("My Sun"), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Himawari-gumi's 1st stage "Boku no Taiyou"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Himawari-gumi 1st Stage"

## h2, yume wo shinaseru wake ni ikanai (让梦想闪耀)

**Definition**: 让梦想闪耀 (“Let Your Dreams Shine”), a SNH48 stage adapted from AKB48 Himawari-gumi's 2nd stage "Yume wo Shinaseru Wake ni Ikanai"

**Notes**: from "AKB48 Himawari-gumi 2nd Stage"

## hammer (锤), 🔨

**Definition**: evidence, proof that a rumor is real

**Notes**: 锤 (chuí) = hammer

## hanged (挂), hang black (挂黑)

**Definition**: publicly blacklisting someone or exposing them of wrongdoing

**Notes**: 挂 (guà) = hang

## hanoi, henei, in the river (河内)

**Definition**: topics or events taking place inside SNH48 Group

**Notes**: 河内 (hénèi) = inside the river

## haoge (豪歌)

**Definition**: GNZ48's yearly singing competition event

**Notes**: 豪歌 (háo gē) = sing boldly

## he, cp he, cphe

**Definition**: a CP that ended on good terms

**Notes**: from "happy ending"

## hefen, pho, river fans (河粉), ihe 盒饭

**Definition**: SNH48 fans, SNH48 Group fans

**Notes**: 河粉 (héfěn) = river fan

## heiliao, black material (黑料)

**Definition**: information that is scandalous or damaging to an idol's reputation

**Notes**: 黑料 (hēiliào) = black material

## hemei, river girls (河妹)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group members

**Notes**: 河妹 (hémèi) = river girl

## hequ, river songs, river music (河曲)

**Definition**: SNH48 Group songs and music

**Notes**: 河曲 (héqū) = river song

## hewai, out of the river (河外)

**Definition**: topics or events taking place outside SNH48 Group

**Notes**: 河外 (héwài) = outside the river

## hexagon fighter (六边形战士)

**Definition**: someone skilled in many aspects of a field, an outstanding all-rounder

**Notes**: from the hexagonal radar charts often used in Japanese media to measure characters' skill attributes

## hezu, he group, river group (河组) 糊组

**Definition**: the main SNH48 discussion group on Douban

**Notes**: 河勿糊组 on Douban

## hgr

**Definition**: Koreans, K-netizens (used to express shock or disapproval at them)

**Notes**: 韩国人 (hán guó rén) = Koreans

## hhhhh

**Definition**: ha ha ha ha ha (laughing)

**Notes**: 哈哈哈哈哈 (hā hā hā hā hā) = hahahahaha

## hjsn, rocket girls (火箭少女)

**Definition**: Rocket Girls 101 (火箭少女 101), a Chinese female idol group

**Notes**: 火箭少女 101 (huǒ jiàn shàonǚ 101) = Rocket Girls 101

## hmt

**Definition**: Hold Me Tight, a SNH48 song

**Notes**: from "hold me tight"

## houbei (後輩), kouhai, hoobae

**Definition**: junior (in terms of age, position, or experience)

**Notes**: 後輩 (hòubèi) = junior

## house, family (家), 🏠

**Definition**: an idol's individual fandom or support organization

**Notes**: 家 (jiā) = house, family

## house collapsed, tafang (塌房)

**Definition**: idol having a serious scandal, usually related to dating

**Notes**: 塌房 (tā fáng) = collapsed house. the idol is the "house"

## house girl (宅女)

**Definition**: female otaku

**Notes**: 宅女 (zháinǚ) = female otaku

## how pay (好 pay), howpay

**Definition**: good match, very suitable

**Notes**: from 好配 (hǎo pèi) = good match

## howe (豪)

**Definition**: great

**Notes**: 豪 (háo) sounds like 好 (hǎo) = good

## hthzkjh

**Definition**: "so sweet, so real, get married soon"

**Notes**: 好甜好真快结婚 (hǎo tián hǎo zhēn kuài jié hūn) = so sweet, so real, get married soon

## hua, flower (花), 🌸

**Definition**: female/feminine appearance or role

**Notes**: often used with "0" or when discussing CPs or member personalities

## i-

**Definition**: fans (of something), lovers (of something)

**Notes**: "i" sounds like 爱 (ài) = love

## idft, idols ft

**Definition**: IDOLS Ft, a streaming-only SNH48 sister group

**Notes**: IDOLS Ft stands for "Idol Studio Ft"

## idol, ouxiang (偶像), aidou, love beans (爱豆), love shake (爱抖露), aidoulu (爱豆露), aidoru

**Definition**: an entertainer, usually young and attractive, whose appeal primarily stems from their public image, personality, or relationship to their fans

**Notes**: 偶像 (àidòu), 偶像 (ǒuxiàng) = idol. also from the Japanese アイドル (aidoru) = idol

## idol otaku, idol house (偶像宅), wota, wotaku, idol wota

**Definition**: otaku that are fans of idols or idol culture, especially Japanese idols

**Notes**: from the Japanese ヲタ (wota)

## immovable c, immovable center, eternal center (不动 c)

**Definition**: an idol who always serves as the center

**Notes**: 不动 c (bù dòng C) = immovable center

## in the dark, in the da (in the 大)

**Definition**: In the Dark, a SNH48 song

**Notes**: 大 (dà) sounds like "dark"

## ins, insta

**Definition**: Instagram

**Notes**: from "Instagram"

## into the pit, enter the pit (入坑)

**Definition**: entering a fandom, becoming a fan of something

**Notes**: 入坑 (rù kēng) = enter a pit

## iqiyi, 271, peach (桃), aqy, 🥝, 🍑

**Definition**: iQIYI (爱奇艺), a Chinese video streaming platform

**Notes**: 二七一 (èr qī yī) = 271, which sounds like "iQIYI" (爱奇艺)

## iron t, tie t (铁 t), iron hoof, iron shoe (铁蹄)

**Definition**: stone butch, butch lesbian, a woman with a very butchy appearance

**Notes**: 铁 t (tiě t) = iron T. 铁蹄 (tiě tí) sounds like "铁 t"
